# 👑 CV King

### Stop sending one CV to fifty jobs. Send fifty A-grade CVs, each one built for the job and aimed at where your career is going.

You know the drill: you find a role you'd be great at, spend an evening rewording your CV, guess at the keywords, export a PDF, and hear nothing back. The applicant tracking system read it before any human did, and it didn't see what the job asked for.

**CV King fixes that in one paste.** Drop in a job link and you get back a CV rewritten for that exact role, checked line by line the way an ATS reads it, scored before and after, with a cover letter ready to send. It's written **only from your real experience**, so every line still holds up in the interview.

> 🖤 A dark, sharp interface inspired by CRED's NeoPOP design system, with award-site page transitions and motion on every screen. Built to feel fast, focused and a little premium, because job hunting is stressful enough.

---

## ✨ Why it works

- 🎯 **Tailored, never invented.** Every CV is built from your master profile. Code checks every number and every skill against it and flags anything new in red.
- 🤖 **Beats the ATS honestly.** You see your keyword match *before*, *after*, and the *ceiling* your profile can truthfully reach, so you know whether a job is worth your evening.
- 🧭 **Aimed at your career goal.** Choose the track (IC Lead, Engineering Manager, Product, Staff, Delivery, Forward Deployed, Solutions, AI Engineer) and the CV leads with what that path values.
- 🌍 **Right for the market.** India, UK, EU, UAE, Singapore, Australia/NZ, US/Canada and Remote each get their own conventions, header line and page length.
- 🔒 **Private by design.** It runs on your laptop. Your CV, the job descriptions and your tracker never leave it, apart from the brief sent to the AI you choose.

## 🚀 Features

### ✂️ Tailor: one paste, one perfect CV
- 🔗 **Paste a link or the full description.** Press Enter for a link, Ctrl+Enter for pasted text, `/` to jump to the box.
- ⚡ **Reads job boards for you** in about a second through their public APIs: Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, SmartRecruiters, Recruitee and Personio. Any other page is read directly.
- 🏷️ **Pulls out the details:** company, role, location, job ID, posted date and the full description.
- ⏱️ **Writes the CV in 1 to 3 minutes**, picking the market and career track from the job's location and title (you can override both).
- 📊 **Keyword match score** with a before / after / ceiling meter, computed by code so the same JD always gets the same score.
- 🔍 **What changed:** headline, summary and every bullet shown against your original, with a count of what was rewritten, merged, kept and left out.
- 🖍️ **Highlight changes on the CV itself**; hover a line to see the original.
- 🎨 **Four designs** (FlowCV serif, Engineer, Modern sans, Classic) and **1 or 2 pages**, re-rendered in seconds with no AI run.
- ✍️ **Edit any section by hand** right next to the live PDF. The same checks run on your edits.
- 🧩 **Gap finder:** requirements your profile doesn't show yet. Tick the ones that are true, they join your profile and the CV re-tailors.
- 💌 **Cover letters in five voices:** Plainspoken, Storyteller, Formal, Warm and Proof points. Keep the one you like.
- 📝 **A 300-character note to the recruiter**, ready to paste.
- ✅ **ATS read-back:** the finished PDF is read back the way an ATS reads it, with a pass/fail checklist.
- 💡 **What a recruiter will notice** about your fit for the role.
- 📥 **One-click download** as `Company_Position_Your_Name.pdf`, then **Mark applied**.

### 🔭 Discover: fresh jobs, ranked for you
- ⭐ **Favourite companies**: add them in Settings by name. The app finds their job board (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workable) or searches the web for their openings. They're scanned first, have their own **Scan favourites** button, and their openings from the last 30 days are pinned at the top of Discover whatever the match.
- 🟦 **Scan Indeed**: searches Indeed for each of your career tracks in your city through Indeed's own Claude connector, reading every posting's full description, so Indeed jobs get real match scores. It runs only when you press it (about a minute, in parallel).
- 🛰️ **Scans all your sources in parallel** (careers pages, job-board searches, web searches) in under a minute.
- 🗓️ **Only fresh postings**, inside a window you choose (24 hours to 30 days).
- 🏆 **Ranked against your profile** by the same match score tailoring reaches.
- 🃏 **Every job as a card** with all its details (posted date, location, area, match bar and why it fits), filters in a column on the left, and cards across the rest of the screen.
- 🎚️ **Smart filters:** seniority (Lead and above, Senior optional), maximum years asked, and skills to skip (for example Java, .NET or C#).
- 📍 **Region and neighbourhood filters**, plus company office locations so "Bengaluru" becomes "HSR Layout".
- 👀 **People to watch:** each scan looks for recent public hiring posts from hiring managers you follow.
- 📦 **Batch tailoring:** tick the jobs you like and tailor them all, a few at a time, in the background.

### 📋 Pipeline: every application, one board
- 🗂️ **Stages in one column, cards across the rest:** All, Tailoring (with live progress), Tailored, Applied, Interviews and Closed, each with a count. Pick one and its applications fill the screen in a wrapping grid, with no long scroll.
- 🔄 **Change status from the card**, or click any card to reopen its CV.
- 📈 **Export to Excel** with one click.

### 🎤 Interview: walk in knowing the questions
- 🔬 **Researches the company first**, on the web with sources: what it actually builds and ships, its stack, its stated values, what it launched lately, and how it interviews for this role (stages, formats and questions candidates report).
- 🧩 **Three rounds, planned like the real thing**: a screen, the deep technical, design, case or managerial round, and a leadership round with **culture fit** built on the company's own values. When the research finds the company's actual process, the rounds follow it.
- 🧑‍💼 **Coding only when the role codes.** Managers, PMs and other non-coding roles get managerial and case questions instead.
- 🎯 **Questions to industry standard** for the JD and level, including deep dives into the claims on your tailored CV, with model answers built only from your real experience.
- 🗣️ **What you could say**: for every question you answer by talking (behavioural, culture fit, managerial, scenarios, your CV), a spoken answer in the first person, built only from your real experience and numbers, and honest where you lack something. The points to hit sit underneath.
- 📈 **Adaptive practice**: one question at a time in a sci-fi HUD. Get two right in a row and the questions get harder; mark one to review and it eases off. Every question has why it's asked, a model answer, follow-ups and pitfalls.
- 💻 **Adaptive coding test (LeetCode-style)** for coding roles: ten problems across five levels, set for the job, in Python or JavaScript. Your code runs live against hidden tests. Solve one and the next is a level harder; skip one and it gets easier. Score, streak and timer included.
- 🛡️ **A real sandbox**: code runs in your browser, in a worker with no network access, never on your machine. Every test is checked against a reference solution first, so a wrong test can't fail you.
- ⌨️ **Keyboard flow**: → next, ← back, Space reveal, G got it, R review again, Ctrl+Enter submit.
- 📥 **Download the notes** as Markdown to read on the way.

### ✉️ Cold mail: get a reply, not a silence
- 🎯 **Pick an application and say who you're writing to**: hiring manager, recruiter, an engineer on the team, or a founder, plus any context you share.
- ✍️ **A short email and its follow-up** (90 to 150 words, then 40 to 70), built from your tailored CV, your real numbers and what the company actually builds. The same checks as your CV flag anything your profile doesn't support.
- 📝 **Edit in place**, then **save to Gmail drafts** through the Gmail connector in Claude. The app never sends: you attach the CV and send from Gmail.
- ⏰ **Follow-up reminders**: mark an email sent and the follow-up is ready when it's due.
- ➕ **Someone new**: for a hiring post or a person you found yourself. Give their name, email and company, plus the job link (read for you) or the post's text. Written from your profile, with a one-click **Tailor a CV for this job**.
- 🔎 **Find contacts** (on every Pipeline card): a web search for the hiring manager, product and engineering leads, HR heads and recruiters for that role, saved under a Contacts accordion with sources. An email is shown only when it's published somewhere, with the page it's on; none are guessed. Each contact has a **Cold email** button that opens Cold mail with their details filled in.

### 🧭 Career: where your next five years can go
- 🗺️ **Your history as an interactive map**, built from your profile.
- 🛣️ **Five or six routes for the next five years**, researched on the web: Forward Deployed / Applied AI, Engineering Manager, Product, Staff IC, Solutions and early-stage.
- 🔎 **Every step explained:** what employers screen for, what you already have, what to build, certifications, projects to take on, and the pay range with its source (no source, no number).
- 💼 **Jobs for any step**, from your scans or a live web search, tailored with one click.
- 🎯 **Set a goal** (for example VP level by a year, at a target pay) and see which routes reach it.
- 💰 **Salary tracker:** log your pay by year to see your growth, where your pace leads, and the yearly growth your goal needs.
- 🌱 **Growth log:** record what you've done, learned or want next. The next map reads all of it, and skills you mark for CVs join your profile.

### 🎬 Motion
- 🌊 **Page transitions** with a two-panel curtain and the screen's name, and the interview's flow of one step at a time, built on GSAP.
- ✨ **Everything arrives with intent:** cards, lists and panels rise in, headings reveal word by word, and scores count up, built on Motion (the framework-free Framer Motion).
- 🚪 **Smooth exits** for the settings drawer, menus and the selection dock.
- ♿ **Respects reduced motion:** if your system asks for less motion, everything appears instantly.

### ⚙️ Writers and settings
- 🧠 **Claude or Gemini**, switched from the top bar. Choose Claude Opus (best writing) or Claude Sonnet (faster).
- 💳 **Runs on your subscription**, through the Claude and Gemini CLIs signed in on your laptop, so there's no API bill.
- 🛠️ **Defaults for every new CV:** market, career track, design, length, writer, jobs tailored at once, and your availability line.

## 🛡️ What it will never do

- 🚫 **Invent anything.** Titles, companies and dates are printed by code, not written by the AI.
- 🚫 **Overstate your title.** The career track changes what your CV leads with, never what it claims.
- 🚫 **Mention a weakness in a cover letter.** Gaps are for your interview prep.

---

### 👥 Profiles: one install, anyone's career
- 📄 **Start from a CV.** Add a profile, drop in the CV (PDF, Word or pasted text) and the app reads it into a master profile, then **checks every number, company and degree against the CV's own text** and flags anything it can't back up.
- 🧭 **It works out your strategy.** From the CV and a web search it decides your field and level, how careers like yours progress, the kinds of role to aim at (your own function, the step up, adjacent moves), the title words that find them, the employers worth watching and the searches worth running.
- 🔁 **Everything follows from that.** Tailor writes to your tracks and level (a Lead keeps "Lead"; an associate never claims "Manager"), Discover ranks against your roles and city, and Interview, Cold mail and Career read the same strategy. Employers with public job boards become Favourites automatically; the rest are one click in Settings.
- 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 **Separate and private.** Each profile has its own CV, applications, scans, goal and salary log. Switch from the top bar; a browser remembers who it was.

## 🏁 Get started

1. 🐍 Install **Python 3.12** and make sure it's on your PATH.
2. 🔑 Sign in to the **Claude CLI** (and optionally run `gemini` once and choose *Sign in with Google*).
3. 📄 Open the app and drop in your CV when it asks. It builds your profile, and you check it before anything uses it. (The first profile can also be written by hand at `data/base_cv.json`; others live under `profiles/`.)
4. ▶️ Double-click **`start.bat`**. The first run sets everything up, then opens http://127.0.0.1:8765.
5. ✂️ Paste your first job link and press **Tailor my CV**.

Keep the `start.bat` window open while you work; closing it stops the app.

## 📁 What's where

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `app/` | The FastAPI server, tailoring engine and web UI |
| `app/static/css/` | The design system: `tokens.css` → `base.css` → `components.css` → `views.css` |
| `app/static/fx.js` | All motion in one place: Motion for components and text, GSAP for page transitions and the interview flow |
| `app/contacts.py` | Finding the people who hire for a role (published emails only, with sources) |
| `app/coldmail.py` | Cold emails and their follow-ups, saved to Gmail drafts (never sent) |
| `app/interview.py` | Company research, the three-round interview plan and the adaptive coding test |
| `app/static/runner.js` | The coding-test sandbox: a network-free worker running JavaScript and Python (Pyodide) |
| `knowledge/playbook.md` | The writing brief: how ATS and recruiters read a CV, and the rules |
| `knowledge/markets.yaml` | Market conventions for each region |
| `knowledge/voices.yaml` | The five cover-letter voices and the banned clichés |
| `app/profiles.py`, `app/strategy.py`, `app/onboarding.py` | Several people on one install; the per-profile strategy that drives every screen; reading a CV into a profile |
| `data/`, `profiles/` *(local only)* | Profiles: each one's CV, strategy, sources, goal and salary |
| `output/` *(local only)* | Per application: PDF, cover letter, JD, report, logo, plus the tracker |
