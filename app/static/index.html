Company offices

Most postings only say "Bengaluru". Note where a company sits and its jobs get that area in Discover. One per line: company | area, e.g. Atlan | HSR Layout or Okta | Embassy Tech Village.

Sources

Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, SmartRecruiters, Recruitee and Personio boards are read through their public APIs. LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed and Glassdoor are refused: their terms forbid automated access.

People to watch

Hiring managers you follow, one per line: name | company | LinkedIn URL. A bare profile URL works too. Each scan looks for their recent public hiring posts. For instant alerts, also switch on the bell (All posts) on their LinkedIn profiles.
