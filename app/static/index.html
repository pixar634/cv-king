Places you'd move to

One per line, city and country, e.g. Amsterdam, Netherlands or Luxembourg. JobSpy searches LinkedIn, Indeed and Google Jobs there too, and Discover shows jobs there whatever the regions above say.

People you know

A referral is the biggest lever on a callback. Download your LinkedIn data (LinkedIn, Settings, Data privacy, Get a copy of your data, Connections) and add its Connections.csv here. It stays in your profile's data folder; nothing is scraped. Discover then marks companies where you know someone, and each application lists them with a referral ask.

The same export has Jobs/Job Applications.csv: add the applications you made on LinkedIn since a date, so your Pipeline and callback rate count them.

Pay floor

Jobs that state pay below your floor are hidden; ones at or above it rank first. Abroad, the work permit's own salary threshold applies too. 0 means no floor.

Few postings state pay. Note how well a company pays (from Levels.fyi or similar), one per line: Databricks | top, Acme | good or Initech | low.

Remote and Europe job boards

Free public job feeds, searched with your career tracks on every scan. Each job links to the board's own posting.

Company offices

Most postings only name the city. Note where a company sits and its jobs get that area in Discover (areas are known for Bengaluru so far). One per line: company | area, e.g. Atlan | HSR Layout or Okta | Embassy Tech Village.

Favourite companies

Scanned first on every Discover run, and their openings are pinned at the top of Discover whatever their match. Type a name and the app finds its job board (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters or Workable); if it has none, its openings are found by web search. A careers-page link works too.

Sources

Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, SmartRecruiters, Recruitee and Personio boards are read through their public APIs. LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed and Glassdoor are refused: their terms forbid automated access.

People to watch

Hiring managers you follow, one per line: name | company | LinkedIn URL. A bare profile URL works too. Each scan looks for their recent public hiring posts. For instant alerts, also switch on the bell (All posts) on their LinkedIn profiles.
